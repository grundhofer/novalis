# Reading Index

The hub for everything I read closely enough to take notes on. One note per source, rated
after finishing, and linked out to whatever research thread it fed. If a book didn't change
how I work, it doesn't get a note — it gets a line in the journal and nothing more.

Two clusters dominate this year: software that respects attention, and the older
maintenance literature that keeps showing up underneath it.

Whatever survives rereading ends up as a claim in the research folder — [[Evergreen Notes]]
is the destination — or as an argument inside [[Atlas Overview]].
