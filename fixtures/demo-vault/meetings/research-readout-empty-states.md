# Research readout — empty states

**Attendees:** Ana Rivera, Mira Colton, Tomas Feld, Priya Raman

Ana presented the empty-state findings ([[Offline-First UX]]) to a wider group than usual, and the discussion was better for it. The central claim — that an empty screen is a teaching surface, not a failure state — survived a fair amount of pushback from Tomas, who reasonably asked whether we're just building an onboarding tutorial in disguise.

The distinction we settled on: a tutorial explains the product, an empty state demonstrates it. The first thing a new vault in [[Atlas Overview]] contains should be real, editable, and deletable, not a modal telling you what you could do.

Mira asked whether demo content confuses people who arrive with an import. Nobody knew. That's the next study.

The thread starts in [[2026-08-05]] and owes most of its framing to
[[The Gardener and the Archivist]] — absence is a design decision either way.
