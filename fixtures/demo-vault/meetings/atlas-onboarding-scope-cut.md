# Atlas onboarding scope cut

**Attendees:** Ana Rivera, Jonah Kessler, Priya Raman

Called specifically to remove things, and it worked. Account linking is out of the Onboarding epic entirely — it existed because an earlier version of the app needed a server, and that requirement went away eight months ago without anyone revisiting the flow that depended on it.

Jonah raised the obvious objection: if we drop it, the eventual sync story has nowhere to live. Fair, but sync isn't in this release, and building the door before the room is how the current flow got three screens too long in the first place. Noted against [[Sync Strategies Compared]] for whenever that work starts.

Net effect: five screens down to three.
