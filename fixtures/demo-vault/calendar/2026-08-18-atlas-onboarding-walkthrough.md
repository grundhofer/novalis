# Atlas onboarding walkthrough

End-to-end run of the shipped onboarding flow from [[Atlas Overview]] on a genuinely fresh machine, with two people from support watching and permitted to interrupt. The build is the release candidate, not a prototype.

If the first-run screen needs explaining out loud, it isn't finished.

Last time we did this the surprises were all in the first thirty seconds — see
[[Atlas Field Test Notes]]. We are watching for hesitation rather than errors — the places where someone stops and reads twice matter more than anything that actually breaks.
