# Commercial licensing

Novalis is free software under **AGPL-3.0-only** ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). For
almost everyone that is the end of it: use it, read it, change it, share it, at
no cost and with no permission needed.

The AGPL asks one thing in return — if you pass Novalis on, or let other people
use a modified Novalis over a network, the people on the other end get the
source. Some plans cannot live with that:

- embedding Novalis, or code taken from it, in a closed-source product;
- shipping a modified Novalis to customers without giving them its source;
- running a modified Novalis as a hosted service without offering its source to
  the users of that service (AGPL section 13);
- redistribution under terms your own legal review will not sign off on.

**A separate commercial license can be negotiated.** Sebastian Grundhoefer is
the sole copyright holder of Novalis's own code, so he can license it to you on
other terms without asking anyone else — which is exactly why contributions
carry the grant described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-licensing).

## Before you write

Two things that make the answer cheap to give:

1. **You may not need this.** Writing a plugin — even a paid, closed-source
   one — is already allowed by the plugin exception in
   [LICENSE](LICENSE), at no cost and with no agreement to sign. See
   [PLUGINS.md](PLUGINS.md#licensing-your-plugin).
2. **Third-party code is not his to relicense.** Novalis statically links
   libgit2, OpenSSL, LibXDiff and others, each under its own terms, and a
   commercial license from him covers Novalis's own code only. Those obligations
   travel with the binary whatever you and he agree. See
   [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
