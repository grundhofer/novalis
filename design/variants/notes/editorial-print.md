## Deviations
1. Traffic lights #FF5F57 / #FEBC2E / #28C840 (.l-close/.l-min/.l-zoom) are the only hex values outside the spec palette and bring green and amber into the sheet. Kept on purpose: the CHROME RULE asks for a native macOS title bar, and in the Tauri app these three dots are drawn by the OS, not by the stylesheet. If the validator rejects extra hexes, replace the three rules with background:transparent;border:1px solid var(--ep-rule-strong) for hollow ink circles.
2. Measure 560px (~82ch at 16px Newsreader) instead of the spec's ~66ch. A true 66ch (~490px) does not fit 26 lines on the 24px baseline in an 800px frame, and 520px already wraps line 17 and clips the tag line; 560px is the widest value that keeps the doc bottom (762) above the status bar (773).
3. l5 column heads: .layout-l5 .col-name letter-spacing .10em and .col-num .12em sit below the spec's 0.14-0.24em band for tracked capitals. Needed so that numeral, "IN ARBEIT" and the count fit a 109px column head with the Roman numerals shown (Rule 7); the full-width board (l4) keeps .22em/.18em.
4. Dark label colour: --ep-label resolves to muted (#9A9080, 5.6:1) in dark rather than ink-soft, so dark labels stay quieter than light ones; the violation named this as an acceptable alternative and the contrast floor is met.
5. Unchanged from before: no "weicher Lichtverlauf" texture (textures are off by the hard rules); tracked uppercase stands in for Kapitälchen; ui-monospace for code; the dark palette is derived, not catalog-verified.

## Violations found by the critic
- [medium/contrast] .style-editorial-print .legend, .tree .board-item .meta, .lang, .palette-prompt::before, .palette-input .kbd, .col-num, .card-when, .board-sub, .result-meta (light mode): --ep-muted #857C6C measures 3.88:1 on paper and 3.59:1 on paper-2, yet it colours 10-11px tracked-caps labels (NAME/GEAENDERT legend, BOARD, JSON, REGISTER, ESC, Roman numerals I-IV) and 13px italic captions (card-when, board-sub, result-meta - the active result sits on paper-2 at 3.59:1). The spec is explicit: muted is decorative/large only; 10-12px small caps that must be read need >= 4.5:1 and should use ink-soft. Dark mode passes (muted 5.62:1). → Switch those selectors to color:var(--ep-ink-soft) (9.09:1 light / 9.10:1 dark). Keep muted only where the spec sanctions or the element is decorative: .marker, .fm .txt, .tree .file .meta (spec says muted), .fence .txt, .result-glyph. Alternatively lighten only in light mode via a --ep-label token that resolves to ink-soft in light and muted in dark.
- [medium/spec] .style-editorial-print .doc{max-width:700px}: At 16px Newsreader the 700px measure holds ~100 characters per line (the paragraph line renders 103 characters before wrapping) versus the spec's 'measure ~66ch' (stated in GRID/SPACING and in HOW NOVALIS LOOKS). Long lines are the classic editorial failure the spec warns about. → Verified in headless Chrome: max-width:560px (~82ch) still fits - doc bottom stays at 762 < 773 with no additional wraps; 520px makes line 17 wrap and pushes the doc bottom to 786, clipping the tag line. A true 66ch (~490px) does not fit 26 lines on the 24px baseline, so use 560px and declare the remaining gap in deviations.
- [low/spec] .style-editorial-print .l-close / .l-min / .l-zoom: #FF5F57, #FEBC2E and #28C840 are the only hex values outside the spec palette and introduce green and amber into a sheet whose signal budget forbids them. The 'native title bar look' justifies them, but HARD RULE 3 requires the palette hexes as given and the validator may reject undeclared extras. → Keep them for the native look but list them explicitly under deviations. If the validator is strict, render hollow ink circles instead: background:transparent; border:1px solid var(--ep-rule-strong).
- [low/fit] .style-editorial-print.layout-l5 .split-board, .split-note{flex:1}: Overriding the base 1.2:1 split to 1:1 gives the board 520px, columns 118px and card content 97px. With the web fonts 'Wochenrueckblick' already measures 99px (2px over), with a fallback serif titles overflow by up to 33px across the column hairline, and card-meta wraps to two rows on 8 of 9 cards. → Restore the base ratio (.layout-l5 .split-board{flex:1.2}) - verified: columns 129-130px, content 109px, no title overflow, tallest column ends at 592, and the note pane still fits (doc bottom 733 < 773 with lines 7, 17 and 20 wrapping). Optionally drop .layout-l5 .card-title to 16px.
- [low/coverage] .style-editorial-print.layout-l5 .col-num{display:none}: Rule 7 says the Editorial style shows Roman numerals in .col-head; the split layout hides them, so l5 loses the style's signature column numbering. → Either keep them visible after widening the board (flex 1.2) with .layout-l5 .col-head{gap:6px} and .layout-l5 .col-name{letter-spacing:.10em}, or state the l5 hide explicitly in deviations.
- [low/spec] .style-editorial-print .tag{letter-spacing:.12em}: Tracked uppercase labels must sit in the 0.14-0.24em band; the 12px uppercase tag is tracked at 0.12em. → letter-spacing:.14em (matches .fm-key and .lang).
- [low/spec] .style-editorial-print .badge-conflict, .tree .conflict .meta: Red marks the current position (.file.active name, meta and square) and, two rows below, the conflict warning (warning glyph plus 'Konfliktkopie' meta). With one accent only, both rows read as highlighted in the screenshot; the spec reserves the accent for counters, hover and the current position. → Keep the warning glyph as the form signal in ink (color:var(--ep-ink)) and the conflict meta italic ink-soft; or restrict red to the glyph alone so the meta text no longer competes with the active row.
- [low/contrast] .style-editorial-print .tree .chev, .tab-close: Both use --ep-rule-strong (#A99E86 = 2.50:1 light, #5A5246 = 2.30:1 dark). The tab close may be quiet by mandate, but the 9px chevron carries folder open/closed state and sits below the 3:1 floor for informative graphics. → color:var(--ep-ink-soft) on .tree .chev (or --ep-muted at 5.6:1 in dark only); leave .tab-close quiet but give it :hover red as already defined.
