## Deviations
1 Light --dn-line-control is rgba(0,0,0,.45) instead of the spec's derived .30. The spec's own a11y rule demands 3:1 for input frames and interactive borders; .30 black on #FAFAFA is 2.10:1, .45 is 3.33:1 (3.30:1 on raised). This is a correction of the spec's internally inconsistent derived light ladder; the dark .36 step is untouched.
2 .btn.ghost keeps border-color var(--dn-line-strong), the decorative hairline (1.33:1 dark), rather than the interactive .36 step. The .04 fill carries the affordance exactly as in the catalog demo's .btn-ghost; a .36 outline would read as an outlined secondary button and add a second border weight to the board header. If the reviewer prefers the strict reading of 'never mix the two roles', the one-line change is border-color: var(--dn-line-control).
3 l5 card titles are 12px, not the suggested 12.5px. Measured with the real fonts, 'Wochenrückblick' is 102.3px at 12.5px/500 (100.7px at 400) and 98.3px at 12px/500, while the reviewer's flex-1.4 title column is 99.2px; at 12.5px the word still broke ('Wochenrückblic / k'). 12px plus card padding 8px 9px and cards padding 5px gives a 103.2px column and 4.9px of spare, verified break-free.
4 l5 code/fence lines are 10.5px (12px .txt padding reduced to 10px) instead of 11.5px. The 56-character JSON line measures 393px at 11.5px and 376px at 11px, both wider than the 388px note pane (which is 390px content, not the ~429px the violation assumed: flex 1.4 gives the note 427px, not 'within ~5px' of the previous width); at 10.5px it is 359px and stays on one line. Prose lines in the pane wrap normally (paragraph 3 lines, quote 2, wikilink line 2).
5 Tab hover colour changed from text-2 to text (a consequence of fix 5: with the resting colour now text-2, the old hover value would have been a no-op).
6 Editor caret sits at end of line while the status bar says 'Sp 4'; the skeleton content is shared and a CSS-only caret cannot be placed between glyphs of proportional text. Flagged, not fixable in the stylesheet.
7 Unchanged from the previous round and still deliberate: accent is teal, not the catalog's Linear violet (per the status rule); film noise, 56px grid, radial gradients and the accent glow are off (per the chrome rule and the spec's own 'RECOMMEND OFF'); the demo kbd's inset shadow and .05 fill are replaced by the ladder's .04 fill with no shadow (depth = layers + hairlines only).
